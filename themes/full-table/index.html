♥ App guide

How to use Lockout Tracker

The app records hand-by-hand scores and calculates the rest automatically.

01 · Getting started

Add players and sign in

1

Add each regular player once. Enter the name and say whether this is your own profile.

2

If it is yours, choose a four-digit PIN, then add an optional bio and picture. Someone added by a friend can choose their PIN later.

3

Sign in to make changes. Select your player name and enter your PIN. The device remembers you until you sign out or switch player.

Any signed-in friend can score an active game. Only the player themselves can edit their profile. The administrator can reset a forgotten PIN or sign everyone out from Lockout Admin.

02 · Start a session

Set up the table

  • Add a title, host and optional notes.
  • Choose any session tags and the false lockout penalty (10 by default).
  • Select everyone playing and start the session.
  • The app is online-only. Wait for the green saved confirmation before moving on.
  • If another device changed the game first, refresh the session before trying again.

03 · During a session

Record each hand

  1. Enter every player's final card total.
  2. Select the player who declared lockout.
  3. The app checks whether the attempt is valid: score 5 or below and strictly lowest.
  4. Add an optional hand note and submit.
✓

Successful lockoutScores 0, unless the declaration is negative.

×

False lockoutScores the declared total plus the session penalty.

=

Other playersKeep their entered hand totals.

Late joiners

Use Add Player in the active session. They join from the current hand, with a starting score equal to the current players' average cumulative score rounded to the nearest whole number. A Joined H# badge identifies them.

Corrections

Edit any hand in the history. Only the most recent hand can be deleted. False lockout detection runs again after an edit.

04 · Past sessions

Review a completed game

Previous Sessions contains every ended game. Open one for the final table, hand-by-hand breakdown, session notes and Cricket Worm and Manhattan graphs.

05 · Statistics

Explore the group

Overall Stats covers session wins, hand wins, rates and averages. Head-to-Head shows direct records when two players shared a session. Detailed Comparison adds shared-session history and wider performance context.

Average Lockout Score means the mean score at which a player declared lockout, including successful and false declarations. Lower is better.

06 · ELO ratings

Read the rating table

  • Every player starts at 1,000.
  • The rating rises when someone finishes higher than expected and falls when they finish lower.
  • A ? marks a provisional player with fewer than 50 hands.
  • Provisional players use K=40; established players use K=24.
  • The 50-hand threshold is checked at the start of each session.
  • Testing sessions do not affect ELO.

07 · Data & privacy

Where the data goes

  • Game data is stored in the administrator's Google Sheet and is visible to anyone with the public app link.
  • Optional photos are resized when needed, then sent to ImgBB and given externally hosted URLs. Initials appear when no picture is used.
  • The app does not sell personal data or use advertising trackers.
  • Edit history records who changed what and when.

Lockout

Declaring that you have 5 points or less at the start of your turn. If successful—strictly lowest—you score 0 for the hand.

False Lockout

A failed attempt where the declared score is greater than 5, or tied with or higher than another player. The score becomes the declared total plus the penalty.

Lockout Score

The actual hand total held when lockout was declared. It is recorded separately even when a successful result scores 0.

Late Joiner

A player joining mid-session. Their starting score is the current players' average cumulative score, rounded to the nearest whole number.

Worm Chart

A line graph showing cumulative scores over time for all players in a session.

Manhattan Chart

A bar chart showing every player's hand-by-hand scores.

Head-to-Head Record

The direct win and loss record between two players when they compete in the same session.

Average Lockout Score

The mean score at which a player declared lockout, including successful and false lockouts. Lower is better.

ELO Rating

A number representing a player's skill level. Everyone starts at 1,000 and moves according to finishing above or below expectation.

Provisional Rating (?)

A rating for a player with fewer than 50 hands. It moves faster to find the player's level, then stabilises.

Player Sign-In

Select your name and enter your four-digit PIN. The device remembers the signed-in player until sign-out or switching.

Session Tags

Categories such as Casual, Holiday, Pub and Tournament. Testing sessions remain in history but are excluded from official statistics, profiles, comparisons, achievements and ELO.

♥ Official game guide

Lockout rules

Win the session with the lowest cumulative score.

K♥

Objective

Keep the total low

Win hands through successful lockouts and finish the complete session with the lowest cumulative score.

Players
2+
Deck
52 cards
Starting hand
4 cards
01

Setup

  1. Choose a dealer; rotate clockwise each hand.
  2. Deal four cards face down to each player.
  3. Arrange them in a fixed formation, commonly 2×2 or 1×4.
  4. Secretly look at any two cards, then return them face down.
  5. Put the remaining deck in the centre. The player left of the dealer starts.

Cards stay in their original positions throughout the hand.

02

Card values

Ace12–10Face valueJack, Queen, King10Red King ♥ ♦−1

Your score is the sum of every card in your hand.

03

Turn sequence

  1. Draw from the face-down pile or the top of the discard pile.
  2. Swap the drawn card with one face-down card, or discard it and leave the hand unchanged.

The first player must draw from the draw pile because no discard pile exists yet.

04

Magic cards

These abilities apply only when drawn from the draw pile.

7Look at one of your own cards8Look at an opponent's card9Blind swap with another player10Open swap with another playerRed JackOptional swap between two other players
05

Slams

Slams can happen at any time, out of turn, when a discarded card matches one on the table.

Your own card

Reveal the match. If correct, discard it and reduce your hand. If incorrect, take a face-down penalty card.

Another player's card

Touch and reveal it. If correct, they take a penalty card. If incorrect, you do.

  • Suits do not matter; red Kings cannot be slammed.
  • You cannot slam on a card you just discarded unless another player makes the top card fresh.
  • Players can defend a card by touching it first.
06

Lockout

When

  • Only at the start of your turn, before drawing.
  • Your total must be 5 or less.
  • Only one player can declare per hand.

Then

  1. Announce Lockout without revealing.
  2. Your hand freezes.
  3. Everyone else gets one final turn.
  4. All players reveal together.
✓

Successful

Your total is 5 or less and strictly lower than every other player. Score 0; keep a negative score.

×

False

Your total is over 5, tied or higher. Score your total plus the session penalty.

07

Winning and table notes

  • The lowest cumulative total across all hands wins the session.
  • The group decides when a session ends.
  • Bluffing and conferring about cards is allowed.
  • Memory matters: positions stay fixed unless a card is removed by a slam.
