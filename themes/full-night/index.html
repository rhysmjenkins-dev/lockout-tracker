App guide

How to use Lockout Tracker

From creating a profile to closing a session and exploring the statistics afterwards.

On this page OverviewGetting startedDuring a sessionPast sessionsStatistics & ELOData & privacy
00

Overview

Lockout Tracker records sessions, hands and player scores. It calculates cumulative totals, lockout results, player statistics, comparisons and ELO ratings.

01

Getting started

Add players

Go to Players → Add new player. Enter a name and say whether the profile is yours. If it is, choose your four-digit PIN and add an optional bio and picture. If it belongs to someone else, they can set their PIN when they first sign in.

Player sign-in

Select your name and enter your four-digit PIN. Your sign-in is remembered on that device until you sign out. Signed-in friends can score games, while each player can edit only their own profile.

Start a session

Add a title, choose the host and players, then optionally add notes, tags, a photo and the false-lockout penalty. Testing sessions remain visible in history but do not affect official statistics or ELO.

02

During a session

Record a hand

Enter one score for every active player and choose who declared lockout. The app determines whether it was successful or false, applies the configured penalty, stores the declared lockout score and updates the live table.

Add a late joiner

A late joiner begins on the next hand. Their starting score is the current players’ average cumulative score, rounded to the nearest whole number.

Edit recent play

Use Edit beside a hand to correct it. The latest hand can also be deleted. Scores and late-join starting totals are recalculated after a correction.

End the session

Check the final totals and choose End session. The result moves to Previous Sessions and official ELO is calculated once.

03

Viewing past sessions

Previous Sessions shows completed and Testing games. Open a session for its final table, metadata, Cricket Worm cumulative graph, Manhattan hand graph and full hand history.

Cricket Worm includes any late-join starting score. Manhattan shows only the score recorded in each individual hand.

04

Statistics and comparisons

Overall Stats includes sessions, shared wins, win rate, hands, average hand score, lockouts and false lockouts. Player names link to profiles.

Head-to-head records compare finishing positions when two players share a session. Detailed Comparison adds performance context. ELO begins at 1000 and moves when a player finishes above or below expectation. A ? marks fewer than 50 official hands.

05

Data and privacy

Profiles and game records are visible to anyone using the app. A personal PIN is required to make changes and a player can edit only their own profile. Do not reuse an important PIN from another service.

Lockout

Declaring that you have 5 points or less at the start of your turn. If successful (strictly lowest score), you score 0 for the hand.

False Lockout

A failed lockout attempt where either your score is greater than 5, or you’re tied/higher than another player. Results in your lockout score + penalty.

Lockout Score

The actual hand total you had when you declared lockout. It is recorded separately for statistics even when a successful lockout scores 0.

Session

A complete game consisting of multiple hands. It ends when the players decide.

Magic Cards

Cards with special abilities when drawn: 7, 8, 9, 10 and the red Jack.

Slam

An out-of-turn action where you match a discarded card with one in your hand or an opponent’s hand.

Late Joiner

A player joining mid-session. Their starting score is the current players’ average cumulative score, rounded to the nearest whole number.

Worm Chart

A line graph showing cumulative scores over time for every player in a session.

Manhattan Chart

A bar graph showing hand-by-hand scores for every player.

Average Lockout Score

The mean score at which a player declared lockout, including successful and false lockouts. Lower is better.

Player Sign-In

Select your name and enter your four-digit PIN. The device remembers the sign-in until you sign out.

ELO Rating

A skill rating beginning at 1000. It rises when you finish higher than expected and falls when you finish lower.

Provisional Rating (?)

A rating marked ? because the player has completed fewer than 50 official hands. It moves faster while finding their level.

Rank-Based ELO

ELO calculated from finishing position rather than raw score. It handles any number of players and ties.

Lockout rules

Get the lowest score by remembering, swapping and shedding the cards in front of you.

01

Objective

Finish the session with the lowest cumulative score. Reduce the value and number of cards in your formation, then declare lockout when you believe you are strictly lowest.

02

Setup

  1. Deal four cards face down to each player in a 2×2 formation.
  2. Each player secretly looks at their first two cards, then returns them to their positions.
  3. Place the remaining deck in the middle and turn one card face up to begin the discard pile.
  4. Cards stay in their original formation positions unless swapped or removed.
03

Card values

A12–10Face valueJ / Q10K♣ K♠10K♥ K♦−1
04

Turn sequence

On your turn, draw either the face-up discard or the top card of the deck. You may swap it with a card in your formation or discard it. A card taken into your formation replaces one card, which goes face up on the discard pile.

Do not look at a face-down formation card unless a magic card allows it.

05

Magic cards

7
Look at one of your own cards.
8
Look at one opponent’s card.
9
Blind-swap one of your cards with an opponent’s.
10
Look at both cards before choosing whether to swap.
Red Jack
Swap cards between two opponents.

Magic powers apply only when the card is drawn from the deck and discarded.

06

Slams

Slamming your own card

When a matching card reaches the discard pile, place your matching formation card on top before play continues. A correct slam removes that card from your hand.

Slamming another player’s card

If you know another player has the match, place it on the discard and give them one of your own cards. They may defend by placing a finger on their card first.

An incorrect slam receives a face-down penalty card. Once the next player discards, the previous card is no longer slammable.

07

Lockout

Declare lockout at the start of your turn when your hand totals 5 or less. Every other player takes one final turn, then all cards are revealed.

Successful lockout

You are strictly lower than every other player and score 0 for the hand.

False lockout

Your score is over 5, tied, or higher than another player. Your declared score plus the configured penalty is recorded.

Other players record the full value of their remaining cards.

08

Winning the game

Play as many hands as the group chooses. Add every hand score together; the lowest cumulative total wins. A tied session shares one win equally between the tied players.
